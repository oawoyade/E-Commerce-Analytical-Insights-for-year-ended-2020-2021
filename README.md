# 🛍️ E-Commerce Analytical Insights Dashboard (2020–2021)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an in-depth analysis of an e-commerce business's performance for the fiscal year ending 2021. The dashboard captures transactional data including product categories, order quantities, shipping costs, sales channels, regional demand, and shipping provider effectiveness. The purpose is to uncover business-critical insights that can guide inventory management, customer experience improvement, cost optimization, and supply chain decisions.

---

## 🗂️ Data Source

The dataset is a structured extract of historical e-commerce sales records. It includes over 1.1 million order line items, capturing key variables such as:
- Product Category
- Product Name
- Country and Postal Code
- Shipping Mode
- Shipping Provider
- Order Priority
- Quantity and Shipping Cost

The data has been anonymized for use in performance analytics and business strategy projects.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Methodology

- **Power BI**: Used for data transformation, DAX-based metric calculations, and visual dashboard creation.
- **Excel**: Applied for pre-processing, data type validation, and sorting of missing or invalid values.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Employed to create dynamic calculations such as shipping cost efficiency, sales by region, and order category shares.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🛒 Product Performance
- **USB Cables** and **Desk Lamps** consistently top product rankings
- Products like **Backpacks**, **White Mugs**, and **Office Chairs** follow closely in volume
- Size preference is skewed toward **M** and **L**, influencing inventory planning

### 🌍 Geographic & Regional Trends
- The UK and France are high-volume sales territories
- Performance is evenly distributed across other EU markets like Portugal, Sweden, and Germany
- Targeted regional campaigns can drive more focused results

### 🚚 Shipping and Fulfillment
- **FedEx**, **Royal Mail**, and **DHL** are nearly tied in shipment volume (~278K units each)
- Balanced use of providers offers flexibility but creates opportunities for rate optimization
- High shipping costs may be consolidated by negotiating volume-based discounts with top providers

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Increase Inventory for Best-Selling Products**
   - Stock up on USB Cables, Kurta Sets, and Home Office Essentials based on sales momentum.

2. **Strengthen Regional Fulfillment Hubs**
   - Given the high volume in top postal codes (e.g., 201301, 122001), local fulfillment centers can reduce shipping delays and costs.

3. **Optimize Shipping Strategy**
   - Evaluate shipping providers for service cost-efficiency—FedEx leads in volume but may not be the most cost-effective.

4. **Tailor Promotions by Country and Channel**
   - Differentiate campaigns for online and in-store based on regional behaviors and order priority trends.

5. **Leverage Size Preferences in Apparel**
   - Concentrate manufacturing and stock for M, L, and XL sizes to align with consumer demand.
